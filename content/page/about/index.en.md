I am building a solid path in systems and network administration.

This portfolio helps me show what I do in practice, but also how I work.

I am not someone who tries to take the most space in a team.

I would rather be reliable, useful, and consistent over time.

## How I work

When I hit a technical issue, my first instinct is to understand it before reacting too quickly.

I like to start from the problem, clarify what I already know, identify what is missing, and then move forward with a clean method.

I fit best in environments where rigor matters, with clear priorities and a serious working structure.

What I want is not only to find an answer.

I also want to keep a clean trace, make the topic easier to read, and be able to explain simply what I did.

## What I bring to a team

In a team, I think I bring stability.

When there is pressure or a problem to handle, I tend to slow things down, put priorities back in order, and move forward steadily.

I care a lot about listening, respect, and keeping communication clear.

I do not like making things more complicated than they need to be.

If a topic can be explained in a simple and useful way, I will always prefer that.

I also think reliability matters as much as speed.

On technical topics, I would rather be serious, coherent, and useful than try to impress too quickly.

## The environments I want to grow in

I naturally see myself in IT environments where service, rigor, and reliability genuinely matter.

Technical support, systems, networks, infrastructure, and operational security are the areas where I can grow seriously and be useful in concrete ways.

I do my best work when the environment is both human and demanding, with a clear structure behind it.

That is where I can learn in a durable way, build good habits, and bring a steady presence to a team.
